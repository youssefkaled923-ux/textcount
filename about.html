About Us

Empowering writers, students, and SEO professionals with fast, private text analysis tools.

Our Mission

At TextCount.net, our goal is to provide a fast, reliable, and privacy-focused utility tool for counting words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in real time. We believe that simple online tools should be completely free, lightweight, and respectful of user data.

Why Choose TextCount?

  • 100% Privacy-First: All text calculations happen client-side in your web browser. Your text is never stored, sent to any server, or saved anywhere.
  • Multilingual Support: Designed to work smoothly across 15+ major global languages.
  • Blazing Fast Performance: Built with clean, modern web technologies to load instantly on both mobile devices and desktops.

Who We Are

TextCount.net was developed as a lightweight utility project dedicated to enhancing digital content creation. Whether you are crafting social media posts, optimizing articles for SEO, or writing academic essays, our tools are tailored to streamline your workflow without distractions.
