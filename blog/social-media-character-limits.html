Complete Social Media Character Limits Guide (2026)

Published: August 2026 • 4 min read

Creating compelling social media copy requires balancing impact with strict character boundaries. Exceeding character limits can cut off key call-to-actions or ruin post formatting.

Key Character Limits by Platform

X (Formerly Twitter)

Free users have a 280-character limit per tweet. Premium subscribers can post up to 25,000 characters. Optimal post length remains between 70–100 characters for maximum engagement.

LinkedIn

Personal posts allow up to 3,000 characters. However, LinkedIn truncates posts after roughly 140 characters with a "...see more" button. Keep your hook within the first 100 characters.

Instagram

Captions support up to 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags. For best results, place your most critical message before the 125-character fold.
