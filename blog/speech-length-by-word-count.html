Speech Length by Word Count: How Many Words Do You Need?

Published: August 2026 • 4 min read

Whether you are preparing a YouTube script, a TED talk, or a university presentation, understanding the relationship between word count and speaking time is crucial.

Average Speaking Speed

The average speaker talks at a rate of 130 to 150 words per minute (WPM). Presentation speeches with pauses typically sit around 125 WPM, while fast-paced video voiceovers hit 160 WPM.
