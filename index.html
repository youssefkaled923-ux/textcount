Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does TextCount.net work?

TextCount.net calculates characters, words, sentences, and paragraphs in real time right in your browser. None of your entered text is stored or sent to any server, ensuring complete privacy.

Why is tracking character limits important for SEO?

Search engines like Google truncate title tags longer than 60 characters and meta descriptions longer than 160 characters. Staying within limits ensures your snippet displays clearly in Search Results.

Is TextCount.net free to use?

Yes, TextCount.net is 100% free with unlimited usage for content creators, copywriters, and marketers.
