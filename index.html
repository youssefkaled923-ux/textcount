Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text kept private?

Yes, 100%. All processing and calculations happen locally inside your web browser. Your text is never transmitted or saved on any server.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM), while speaking time uses an average pace of 130 WPM.

Why is keyword density important for SEO?

Monitoring keyword density ensures your content remains natural and focused without triggering search engine penalties for keyword stuffing.
